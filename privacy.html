Privacy Policy for RIFT (com.game.rift)

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RIFT (package ID: com.game.rift, "the Game") collects, uses, and protects user and device data in compliance with Google Play User Data policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous Device / Guest Identifiers: When you select "Play as Guest," the Game generates a random, anonymous User ID (UID) stored locally on your device and synced via Google Firebase to preserve your in-game progression, ship loadouts, Syndicate affiliation, and career leaderboard statistics.
  • Optional Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google to sync your profile across multiple devices, we receive your basic Google account identifier solely for authentication and cloud save synchronization.
  • Gameplay & User-Generated Content (UGC): We store your user-selected Pilot Callsign, Syndicate/Armada tags, custom ship visual recipes, and match telemetry (bounties, kills, wins). In-game reporting and player-blocking tools are provided to moderate user-generated callsigns and chat.

2. How We Use & Share Information

We use the collected information exclusively to operate core game functionality, including live multiplayer arena synchronization, leaderboards, Syndicate rosters, and account recovery. Data is processed using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or share personal or sensitive user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between the Game and cloud services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Players can clear local guest data at any time through their Android device settings (Settings ➔ Apps ➔ RIFT ➔ Clear Storage). To request permanent deletion of cloud-synced account or leaderboard data associated with your UID or Google account, contact the developer using the support email address listed on the official Google Play Store listing for RIFT (com.game.rift).
