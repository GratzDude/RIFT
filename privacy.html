This Privacy Policy describes how Threshold Dynamics Studio, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles user and device information in NEXUS (Android package ID: com.game.rift, "the Game") in accordance with Google Play Developer Program and User Data policies.

1. Pilot Modes & Information We Collect

  • Optional Google Sign-In (Sworn Pilot Cloud Sync): If you choose to sign in via Google Credential Manager as a Sworn Pilot, we receive your Google-authenticated account UID via Google Firebase Authentication solely to authenticate your session and synchronize your Pilot Callsign, Live & Peak Resonance, Syndicate / Strike Wing tags, custom hull/visual configurations, Sworn Pilot Cloud Leaderboard records, live sector presence, and Subspace Comms messages via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • [RFT] Drifter Sessions (Play Without Signing In): If you choose Enter as [RFT] Drifter (Local Session), your session runs strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Drifter profiles are never uploaded to public Cloud Leaderboards and do not create a cloud account unless you later choose to sign in with Google.
  • On-Device Tactical AI & Audio Synthesis: The in-game #nexus-ai tactical console, procedural 3D hull/visual forge, and real-time audio synthesizer execute locally on your device without transmitting personal prompts or microphone/camera recordings. The Game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise GPS location.

2. User-Generated Content (UGC) Moderation, Reporting & Blocking

Sworn Pilots who participate in multiplayer sectors, Syndicates, or Subspace Comms display their chosen Pilot Callsign, Syndicate tag, and chat messages to other signed-in players. To maintain a safe competitive environment, NEXUS enforces:

  • Automated Profanity & Format Filtering: All Pilot Callsigns, Syndicate tags/mottos, custom room titles, and Subspace Comms transmissions pass through automated profanity sanitization and strict Firestore security rule validation.
  • In-Game Pilot Reporting & Instant Blocking: Players can report abusive callsigns or Subspace Comms messages and immediately block any pilot directly inside SUBSPACE COMMS and SYNDICATE / STRIKE WING (Syndicate Citadel), immediately hiding blocked UGC on the client.

3. Beta Tester Economy & Dormant Commercial Billing

During the current Beta test phase, commercial real-money Google Play Billing is dormant and disabled in live gameplay. Pilots are provisioned with a non-depleting 999,999 tester Rift Shards balance strictly for testing cosmetic trims, finishers, and modular Frame bays. Zero real-money charges or payment card details are collected by Threshold Dynamics Studio, LLC while commercial billing is dormant.

4. How We Use & Protect Information

Information synced by signed-in Sworn Pilots is used exclusively to operate core multiplayer features, real-time Overwatch/arena synchronization, Syndicate rosters, and competitive leaderboards on Google Firebase infrastructure. All network traffic between NEXUS and Google Firebase is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. We never sell, rent, or share user data with third-party advertisers, ad networks, or data brokers, and the Game contains zero third-party advertising SDKs.

5. Data Retention, Account Sign-Out & Deletion

  • Local Session & Reset Controls: You can sign out, reset local pilot preferences/loadouts via in-game HELM SYSTEMS and STATS & CODEX, or clear all local app storage at any time in Android system settings (Settings > Apps > NEXUS > Storage & cache > Clear storage).
  • Cloud Account & Leaderboard Deletion: Sworn Pilots can sign out inside HELM SYSTEMS at any time. To request permanent deletion of cloud-synced Sworn Pilot dossier, Syndicate, and leaderboard records associated with your Google Firebase UID, contact Threshold Dynamics Studio, LLC via the developer support email listed on the official Google Play Store page for NEXUS (com.game.rift). Verified deletion requests are processed promptly.
